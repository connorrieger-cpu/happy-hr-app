# Ossington Ave, north end (210 Ossington to Dundas St W): happy hour specials at seven venues

Research date: 2026-10-26 (a Monday). All prices are CAD, as printed by each source. "Accessed" means the page carries no visible publish date, so the only date is the day I read it.

## Per-venue records: status, happy hour, days, start/end times, specials and prices

### Takeaway
Only two venues publish a real time-boxed happy hour. Manita runs Mon–Thu 3–6 pm with full prices on its 2026 menu. Bar Libretto runs 4–6 pm and 9 pm–late but publishes no special prices. The Haifa Room has no happy hour but runs two all-day drink specials on its own July 2026 menus: $6 arak every day, and half-off wine bottles on Tuesdays. Bar Libretto also has two bottle deals: half-off bottles on Wednesdays (menu) and on Sundays (website). Salt Wine Bar, La Banane (and the upstairs Bar Banane), Mickey Limbo's and Communist's Daughter publish no happy hour anywhere I could find. Record them as "no happy hour found".

## Is each venue still open? (closures, moves, renames, rebrands; Bar Libretto format)

### Takeaway
None of the seven is reported closed. Two have changed identity. The original Pizzeria Libretto at 221 Ossington is now Bar Libretto, a late-night bar format since Feb 20, 2026. The Dakota Tavern at 249 Ossington is now Mickey Limbo's, soft-opened June 11, 2026. The other five show current operation on their own sites or in recent listings. For Salt Wine Bar and the Communist's Daughter, that evidence is weaker.

### Takeaway
