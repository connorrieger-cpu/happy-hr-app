# Ossington happy hour specials: roundups, new spots and closures

Research date: 2026-10-26. Scope: Ossington Ave from Queen St W to Dundas St W, plus the corners of Queen and Dundas. All prices are CAD and are copied from the sources. Several OpenTable pages timed out three times and could not be read (see Gaps).

## Section A: Which Toronto happy-hour roundups (2025–2026) mention Ossington venues, and what do they say?

### Takeaway
Roundup coverage of Ossington is thin. Only a few tracked venues come up: Azhar Kitchen + Bar (most often), Central, and Bar Libretto/Don't Tell (opening and rebrand news only, no happy hours). The most detailed recent figures are Azhar's own menu and an aggregator that re-posted that menu in October 2026. The "2026 Edition" Fringinto roundup was first published in June 2025 and includes some stale entries (for example Electric Bill, which closed in August 2026).

## Section C: Have any tracked venues closed, moved or rebranded in 2025–2026? (plus other strip churn)

### Takeaway
**La Cubana (tracked) has closed.** Its Ossington location (92 Ossington Ave) shut in 2026, all its permanent locations have since closed, and Company Bagel is taking over 92 Ossington. Two other tracked venues are recent changes rather than closures. Bar Libretto is the February 2026 rebrand of Pizzeria Libretto's original Ossington site. Don't Tell is a new bar that opened around July 2026. I found no closure reports for the other tracked venues, though I did not check each one individually.

### Cited Findings

**Tracked venues**
- **La Cubana: CLOSED (Ossington and all permanent locations).** blogTO (posted about September 2026) says the Ossington location closed first, and the last permanent location, at The Well, closed a few months later. The Roncesvalles flagship closure was announced in September 2025. The owners cited the pandemic, inflation, food costs, taxes, insurance, energy increases and weather events. No exact Ossington closing date is given. — [blogTO, Sept 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/09/la-cubana-toronto-closed/)
  - **Replacement:** Company Bagel is coming to 92 Ossington Ave, described as the former home of La Cubana's "last remaining brick-and-mortar." No opening date has been announced. — [blogTO, Sept 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/09/company-bagel-toronto/)
  - **Minor conflict:** the Company Bagel article calls 92 Ossington La Cubana's "last remaining" location, while the La Cubana article says The Well closed after Ossington.
- **Bar Libretto: rebrand.** Pizzeria Libretto's original Ossington site was rebranded as Bar Libretto, effective Feb 20, 2026. The menu is cut back to pizza plus snacks, with DJs Fri/Sat, the kitchen open until midnight and last call at 2 a.m. Toronto Life and Whatnow covered the change; the search summary attributed the Feb 20 date to blogTO. — [Toronto Life](https://torontolife.com/food/pizzeria-libretto-bar-ossington/); [Whatnow](https://whatnow.com/toronto/restaurants/pizzeria-libretto-rebranding-original-ossington-site/)
- **Don't Tell: new opening (July 2026).** blogTO describes a "hush-hush new bar" that just opened on Ossington. I saw only the headline and URL. — [blogTO, July 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/07/dont-tell-bar-toronto/)
- **Central: opened July 3, 2025** (so it is a 2025 opening, not a closure). — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/18/3101308/0/en/Central-Ossington-Opens-July-3-Toronto-s-Newest-Spot-for-Food-Cocktails-and-Culture.html)
