# Ossington happy hour specials: 136 to 207 Ossington Ave (Reposado, Favorites Thai, Paris Paris, Bobbie Sue's, Linny's, Superpoint, Foxley)

Research date: 2026-10-26. All "fetched" checks below were made on that date. "Snippet" means the fact came from a search-engine summary and the page itself was not fetched. That makes it weaker evidence.

## Does each venue run a happy hour or daily special, and when? (per-venue records)

### Takeaway
None of the seven venues has a formal, dated "happy hour" with set start and end times on its own website. The only specials on a venue's own site are two undated posts on Favorites Thai's homepage: a Wednesday all-night half-price deal on select wine bottles, and a patio-only deal with Singha beer and $10 wine. Reposado has one undated third-party lead: "Tequila Tuesdays" with $6 Palomas. Superpoint has been closed since August 24, 2025. For the app, record six venues as "no happy hour found." Favorites Thai's specials should be recorded as low-to-medium confidence because they are undated.

### Inferences
- In the app, Superpoint should be hidden or marked closed rather than shown with a happy hour.
- Favorites Thai's "Wine Wednesday" is the only special any venue itself advertises that could fit a "happy hour now" listing. It applies Wednesdays from opening at 6 pm until close at 10 pm. Because the post has no date, the app should flag it as "unverified, may have ended" or leave it out. The patio deal with Singha and $10 wine reads as a one-off event and should not be stored as recurring.
- The dead website domains at Reposado and Foxley weaken confidence that they are still open. Foxley has at least a Feb 2026 OpenTable review. Reposado has nothing newer than an Apr 2025 listing.
- Paris Paris and Linny's publish full current menus with no happy hour, so "no happy hour" is a well-supported value for both.

- Paris Paris: the "Promoción nocturna" tag on OpenTable could not be checked because the OpenTable pages timed out. The Dinner and World Cup PDFs were not read; only the All Day PDF was.
- Bobbie Sue's: I found no 2023–2026 source confirming it is open or closed.
- Linny's: hours conflict across sources, and the official site shows none.
- Foxley: there is no venue-owned menu or hours, and the OpenTable page timed out, so its details come only from search snippets.
- Instagram accounts, which these venues most likely use to post specials, were not checked because Instagram cannot be fetched with these tools.
