# Ossington Ave happy hour specials, south end (13 to 90 Ossington Ave, Toronto ON)

Research date: 2026-10-26. Scope: seven venues between Queen St W and roughly Argyle St. All prices CAD.

Method note (read first): Each venue's own website was checked where one exists. OpenTable venue pages timed out on every WebFetch attempt (dont-tell-toronto, koukla-toronto-2, mamakas-taverna-toronto, the Ossington happy-hour feature page). For OpenTable I only have search-engine snippets. sweatybettysbar.com returned HTTP 429 twice and Yelp returned HTTP 403. Instagram was not checked. "No happy hour found" below means "not on the venue's own site or any source I could reach". It does not prove the venue has none.

## 1. Sweaty Betty's, 13 Ossington Ave (dive bar)

### Takeaway
Open, but no current, dated happy hour could be verified. The only specific claim is from an undated Yellow Pages patio guide: "weeknights between 5 and 7 pm, highballs and pints are a dollar off." Treat it as unverified and possibly years out of date.

Structured record:
- Status: open (multiple 2026-dated listings; no closure reports)
- Happy hour: unclear. Listings say it has one, but no dated source gives terms.
- Days: "weeknights" (undated source only; which weekdays is not specified)
- Start: 17:00 (undated source only)
- End: 19:00 (undated source only)
- Specials: highballs, $1 off; pints, $1 off (undated source; no base prices given)
- Confidence: low

### Cited Findings
- Undated patio article, quoted exactly: "They offer great drink specials (weeknights between 5 and 7 pm, highballs and pints are a dollar off)". The article has no publish date. The only dates on the page are the site footer "Copyright © 2026" and a site build tag "rev 20260930.1535", which describe the website, not the article. — [Yellow Pages, The top patios in Toronto: West end edition](https://www.yellowpages.ca/pl/the-top-patios-in-toronto-west-end-edition-P3866)
- Apple Maps lists "happy hour" as a feature of the bar but gives no times or deals (search snippet only). — [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?place-id=I1B19A131D9DEA9EA)
- An Evendo guide says the bar has happy hour drink specials, with no details (search snippet only, undated). — [Evendo](https://evendo.com/locations/canada/golden-horseshoe/bar/sweaty-betty-s)
- A page marked "Updated June 2026" does not mention happy hour. It gives regular prices of shots about $6.25 and mixed drinks about $7.25 to $8.25, not tied to a time window. — [TopTorontoClubs](https://toptorontoclubs.com/clubs/sweaty-bettys-bar-toronto/)
- blogTO, posted June 26, 2018, gives the same regular prices: Pickleback $6.25, Greyhound $7.25, Dark & Stormy $8.25. No happy hour is mentioned. — [blogTO bar listing](https://www.blogto.com/bars/sweaty_bettys/)
- An undated one-off promo: $6 pints of Creemore Springs Lager all week for the 15th anniversary, with $2 per pint going to Sistering. This was an event, not a recurring happy hour. — [blogTO events](https://www.blogto.com/events/sweaty-bettys-15th-anniversary-toronto/)
- Hours conflict across third-party sources (all from search snippets):
  - blogTO patio guide: Mon–Thu 5 pm–2 am, Fri–Sun 3 pm–2 am — [blogTO patio guide](https://www.blogto.com/patio-guide/patio/sweaty_bettys/)
  - Travel Gay: weekdays 15:00–02:00, weekends 13:00–02:00 — [Travel Gay](https://www.travelgay.com/venue/sweaty-bettys)
  - The Gay Passport: weekdays from 3 pm, weekends from 1 pm, to 2 am — [The Gay Passport](https://thegaypassport.com/explore/toronto-gay-bars/)
  - Apple Maps, per snippet: 1 pm–2 am weekdays, 3 pm–2 am weekends, which is the reverse pattern — [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?place-id=I1B19A131D9DEA9EA)
- Described as a long-standing queer-owned dive bar at the foot of the Ossington strip. Recent events include a monthly vinyl session and weekly tarot. The listing shows "No upcoming events scheduled". — [ma.to](https://ma.to/venue/sweatybettysbar)
- The official site is listed as sweatybettysbar.com. Phone 416-535-6861. — [Scoundrel's Field Guide](https://scoundrelsfieldguide.com/ontario/toronto/sweaty-bettys/); [NOW Toronto](https://nowtoronto.com/music/sweaty-bettys/)
- I could not load the official site: [sweatybettysbar.com](https://www.sweatybettysbar.com) returned HTTP 429 on two attempts, so its content is unverified.

### Inferences
- The TopTorontoClubs "June 2026" prices match blogTO's 2018 prices exactly. They are probably copied, so they are not evidence of 2026 prices.
- A $1-off weeknight 5–7 pm deal is plausible for this bar, and two listings flag "happy hour" as a feature. Still, no dated or first-party source confirms the current terms. For the app, show it at most as "unverified" or leave it out.

### Gaps
- Whether the 5–7 pm weeknight deal is current, which weeknights it covers (Mon–Thu or Mon–Fri), and the base prices of highballs and pints. Checking the official site, the Instagram, or calling would settle this.
- Exact current opening hours, since sources conflict.
- The Yellow Pages article's publish date.
