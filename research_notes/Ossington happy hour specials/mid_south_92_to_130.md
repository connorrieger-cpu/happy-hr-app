# Ossington Ave happy hours, 92 to 130 Ossington (8 venues), researched 2026-10-26

Method note: venue-owned sites were fetched directly where possible. bellwoodsbrewery.com and OpenTable blocked or throttled automated fetches, so they were read in a real browser pane on 2026-10-26. lacubana.ca now returns a Squarespace "Private Site" page. soostoronto.com fails TLS (its certificate is Squarespace's, not the venue's). cdbossington.com, the old Côte de Boeuf domain, now serves unrelated adult content, so the app must not link it. Times below are local Toronto time (24h in brackets). Prices are CAD as printed by the source, before tax and tip. "Accessed" means the page showed no publish or update date, so the record carries the date I read it.
