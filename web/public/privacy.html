Happy HR privacy

Updated October 28, 2026 · applies to the iPhone app and happy-hr-beta.pages.dev

Happy HR shows happy hours and daily specials in Toronto neighbourhoods, Guelph and Burlington. You can use it without an account. We keep only what the features you use need, we don’t show ads, and we never sell or share your information.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to work out walking times to each spot and to open the area you’re in; that happens on your phone. To see where Happy HR gets used, it sends us only a rough square of the map you opened it in: about half a kilometre across near the areas we cover, about five kilometres anywhere else. Never your exact spot, and never linked to you or your account. The home-screen widget uses the last place the app saw you, on your phone.

How the app is used

To learn what’s useful, the app and the website count, without names or accounts: which area is opened, that rough square (only if location is on), and which buttons are tapped (opening a spot, search tiles, directions and so on). We keep daily totals. To count how many phones used it each day, we also keep a scrambled version of the app’s random ID for 90 days. Only the owner can see these counts.

If you make an account

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an ID for you, and an email only if you choose to share it (it can be a private relay address).
  • Sign in with email: your email address, so we can send you a 6-digit code. Codes and sign-in tokens are stored scrambled (hashed), never as is.
  • Your favourites, so they follow you to another phone.
  • Your chat name and messages. The group chat is public to everyone using Happy HR. If you block or report someone, we keep that so it works and so we can review reports.

You can delete your account in the app (Account › Delete account). That removes your account, favourites, chat messages, blocks and reports straight away, and ends your Sign in with Apple link.

Adding deals and checking in

When you suggest a deal or say whether one worked, we store it with a random ID made by the app (not linked to you or your account) and a scrambled version of your network address, to stop spam and limit how many a day anyone can send. Deals you add are shown to everyone.

Venue owners

If you ask to manage a venue’s page, we keep the name, role and contact details you give so we can check it’s really you.

Without an account

Favourites, reminders and settings are kept on your phone (or in your browser on the web), apart from the anonymous counts above. Reminders are notifications your phone schedules itself.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs the website and stores the data above.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes by email.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and the app’s maps.
  • Google provides the website’s map and venue photos. The app asks Google for a venue’s photo by the venue’s place ID only; nothing about you is sent.

Questions

Happy HR is in beta. If you’re testing it, send us a note with TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback, and we’ll answer questions or delete anything we hold about you.
