# King West: 41 spots with deals, 65 listed

Checked on **Wednesday, October 28, 2026**. Of the 86 places in `candidates.json`, 65 are real sit-down bars and restaurants that serve alcohol, and all 65 were researched; the other 21 are buildings, duplicates, fast food, takeout, a dispensary, a food truck or places with no drinks (Table D). About 15 more places from the excluded list, guides and news were checked for the boundary and for wrongly excluded bars, so roughly 100 places were looked at in all.

**41 spots have deals worth loading**: 40 from the venue’s own website, menu PDF, menu image or programming page (all current, 2026, apart from Cibo’s July 2024 late-night price sheet, which the current site still links), and 1 reported (The Wheat Sheaf’s Monday wings, from a guide listing). Seven of the 40 publish times but no prices (Chamberlain’s, Public Gardens, Café Renée, The Keg, Harriet’s cocktails, Casa Madera, 1 Kitchen’s spritz hour) and are loaded with `"specials": []` or a “special prices” item and a note. **24 more spots** with no deals make up the rest of the list, for **65 listed** (`deals.json` 41, `directory.json` 24).

King West is the opposite of Ossington: almost every big room publishes a happy hour, usually on its own site, and most run two windows (afternoon and 9 or 10 PM to close). The standouts:

- **Rodney’s Oyster House**: $2.50 “Low Tide” oysters, $6 cans and $9 Sealegs, Mon–Fri 3–6 PM.
- **The Parlour**: $3 East Coast oysters, $8 margaritas, highballs and Tippy Canoe pints, $7 lobster rolls, open to close Sunday–Thursday (its new hours from September 2026).
- **Añejo**: $7.50 margaritas, $4 tacos and half-price tequila, 3–5 PM and 10 PM–close every day.
- **Petty Cash**: $7 mixed drinks and beer bottles with half-price apps 5–8 PM daily (all day Monday and Tuesday), $5 rail on Thursdays till midnight and $1 house wine by the ounce every day.
- **King Taps**: $5 Crown Lager and highballs, $6 house wine, open to 6 PM on weekdays, plus half-price wine on Tuesdays.

Other strong ones: Silent H ($10 margaritas, $4 tacos), Oretta’s aperitivo ($7 wine), Lulu Bar and Bridgette Bar at The Well ($5 rail after 9 PM Thursday–Saturday), GRETA’s $5.50 Sundays and WVRST’s weekday beer-hall specials.

One limitation runs through everything: Instagram and Facebook can’t be read, OpenTable refused every automated request, and several sites (The Wheat Sheaf, Toro Taquería, Century, Miss Likklemore’s) returned nothing readable. “No deals found” means none in any source that could be reached.

## Method

Every spot was checked in RESEARCH.md’s order: its own site (happy hour pages, menu PDFs and images, events and “weekly programming” pages, Toast and Sociavore menus), then its booking page (OpenTable refused automated requests, so booking pages were only seen as search snippets), then guides (Toronto Life’s December 2025 oyster-deals list, Streets of Toronto, blogTO, Curiocity, Fringinto’s “2026 edition” list, The Well’s own pages), then a web search for “<name> happy hour”, “specials” and “wing night”. PDFs were read with PDFKit and image menus were viewed directly. Times are Toronto time; prices are CAD before tax and tip, as printed. Days and close times follow the venue’s own hours where they differ from Google’s.

Boundary: King St W from Spadina to Tecumseth, Adelaide to Wellington, using the polygon in `area.ts`. Addresses near the edges were geocoded (OpenStreetMap) and tested against it: 440 King W (Hey Lucy) and 430 King W fall east of Spadina, 788 King W (Sonny Can’t Dance) west of Tecumseth, and 51 Camden St (the Ace Hotel) north of Adelaide, so they’re out.
