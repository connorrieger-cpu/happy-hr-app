# Guelph: 44 spots publish deals, and the best value sits in the pubs and the chain bars

On Wednesday, October 28, 2026, all 198 Google Places candidates in `candidates.json` were triaged against the listing rules. **75 were left out** (fast food and takeout counters, food courts, stores, places with no alcohol service, a strip club and a few closed or private rooms; Table D), and **the other 123 were checked for happy hours and daily specials**, downtown first. **44 spots have deals to load: 38 from their own or their chain’s site or menu (`venue`) and 6 from a third-party guide only (`reported`)**. The area lists **91 spots**: the 44 with deals (`deals.json`) and 47 of the best of the rest (`directory.json`), weighted to downtown and to well-loved local bars and restaurants.

**Downtown runs on day-of-the-week specials rather than a classic 4–6 pm happy hour.** McCabe’s, 1881, Royal Electric, Jimmy Jazz, Trappers Alley, La Cucina (both rooms), Miijidaa, The Wooly and Baker Street Station each have a deal for most nights of the week, and only McCabe’s (its “24 deal”), Trappers ($1 wings 2–5 pm), 1881 (half-price apps Wed–Sun 2–5 pm), The Wooly ($6 pints) and The WORKS run time-boxed windows. The classic two-window happy hour (around 2–5 pm and 9 pm–close) belongs to the south-end and Stone Road chains: Milestones, Scaddabush, State & Main, Kelseys, St. Louis, Shoeless Joe’s, Symposium and Chuck’s Roadhouse.

**The five best deals:**

1. **St. Louis Bar & Grill (202 Clair Rd E):** $4 bar rail, 5 oz house wine, 12 oz domestic draught, domestic bottles, sliders, pickle coins and fries, every day from open to 5 pm and 9 pm to close (dine-in); half-price wings Tuesdays ([St. Louis $4 happy hour](https://www.stlouiswings.com/happyhour2025/), Aug 2026; [Guelph page](https://locations.stlouiswings.com/on-guelph-5247/)).
2. **McCabe’s Irish Pub & Grill (9 Wyndham St N):** the “24 deal”, 12 wings, fries and a pitcher of domestic beer for $24, daily 2–4 pm and 9 pm to close; 49¢ wings Mondays after 8 pm and a $18 pound-and-a-pint on Thursdays ([McCabe’s Guelph](https://www.mccabes.ca/guelph)).
3. **Piper Arms (515 Woodlawn Rd W):** $5 20 oz domestic pints every day 11 am–4 pm and after 9 pm, plus a late-night menu after 9 pm (half-price apps, 99¢ wings, $5 rail, $6 house wine) and 99¢ wings all day Tuesday ([daily specials PDF](https://piperarms.com/storage/2026/04/Daily-Specials_2026-1.pdf), Apr 2026; [Guelph drinks menu](http://piperarms.com/storage/2026/05/Guelph-1.pdf), May 2026).
4. **Fork On York (648 York Rd):** every day 2–5 pm, $6 Sleeman draft, $11 margaritas, spritzes, mules and Caesars, $13 apps and a $15 pound of wings; half-price bottles all night on Wednesdays ([happy hour](https://forkonyork.com/promotions/); [Wine Wednesdays](https://forkonyork.com/wine-wednesdays/)).
5. **1881 Steakhouse & Burger Bar (72 Macdonell St):** half-price appetizers Wed–Sun 2–5 pm, 69¢ jumbo wings and $4 Cuervo on Tuesdays, half-price burgers Thursdays and half-price wine bottles Fridays after 4 pm ([1881 daily specials](https://www.1881steakhouseandburgerbar.com/)).

Close behind: The Shakespeare Arms ($5 domestic pints weekdays 11 am–5 pm, 90¢ wings Tuesdays), Brass Taps on campus ($5.95 pints all day Thursday, $5.95 cocktails Monday), Borealis ($10 apps and $6 taps, Mon–Fri 2–5 pm and Thu–Sat 8 pm–close), Kelseys ($5 beer, wine and mixed drinks daily 3–5 pm and 8 pm–close), Bad Luck’s Karaoke Sundays (everything $5) and Trappers Alley’s $1 wings 2–5 pm every day.

**What couldn’t be read.** Instagram and Facebook couldn’t be read, and several downtown bars post specials only there (PJ’s says so outright; Bad Luck, Royal Electric, Jimmy Jazz and The Wooly point to theirs). OpenTable pages didn’t load, Beertown’s site sits behind a Cloudflare bot check (not worked around), Yelp blocks automated reading, and web search returned almost nothing for Guelph pubs. Five sites were down or empty (Capistrano Bistro, W Sushi, Super Happy Garden, Cafe Greek Garden and Cherry Blossom). “No deals found” therefore means none appeared in any source that could be reached, not that a spot has none.

## Method

- **Candidates.** `candidates.json` (Google Places sweep, October 28, 2026) lists 198 places. 75 were left out under the listing rules (Table D). Chains were kept and researched like any other spot, except the fast-food and diner chains the brief names (A&W, Pizza Hut, Denny’s) and counter-service chains like barBURRITO.
- **Order of checking.** For each remaining spot: its own site (home, specials, events, drinks and menu pages, PDF and image menus), then the chain’s Guelph location page for chains, then local and third-party sources, then a web search. Downtown (St. George’s Square, Wyndham, Macdonell, Carden, Quebec and Cork) was done first, then the Ward and Gordon St, then Stone Rd and the university, the west end (Woodlawn, Silvercreek, Paisley, Speedvale), the east end (York, Eramosa, Victoria) and the south end (Clair, Gordon, Kortright).
- **Sites that need a browser.** Wix, Square Online, Sociavore and the Recipe chains’ location pages (Kelseys, Montana’s, East Side Mario’s, Swiss Chalet, State & Main) render their specials with JavaScript, so they were read in a real browser; Scaddabush’s and St. Louis’s Guelph menus, The Real Deal’s specials and Fork On York’s happy hour were read the same way. Several venues publish specials only as images (Bad Luck, NV, Chuck’s, St. Louis, Shoeless Joe’s, Piper Arms); each image was opened and read, and dates come from the upload path or the file’s Last-Modified date where there is one.
- **Third-party source.** The one local guide with real coverage is **Cheap Eats Guelph** ([cheapeatsguelph.com](https://cheapeatsguelph.com/)), a free local deal calendar written up by the Guelph Mercury. Its data file (`deals.json`, generated October 9, 2026) gives each deal a source (venue website, owner or staff submission, customer submission) and a “verified” date. It was used to cross-check venue deals and to find deals that venues don’t publish themselves; a deal that rests on it alone is graded `reported`. GuelphToday, Explore Guelph and the Downtown Guelph business association had no current happy-hour roundups that could be found.
- **Grading.** `venue` means the venue’s or chain’s own site, menu or location page, current in 2025–2026. Chain-wide offers (The WORKS, St. Louis, Chuck’s Roadhouse, Wild Wing, Piper Arms, Shoeless Joe’s, Boston Pizza) are noted as such; where the chain has a Guelph page, it was checked. Where a venue-graded spot also has a deal or price that only Cheap Eats gives, it is loaded with a note saying so (The Wooly’s Thursday ribs, Bardō’s happy hour, Wild Wing’s food prices). Set menus at regular value (Bar Mason’s $50 bottomless brunch, Cherry Blossom’s $42 all-you-can-eat sushi, Royal City Restaurant’s combination plates) are not counted as deals, nor are takeout-only offers (Bardō’s $10 off large pizzas).
- **Times** are 24-hour Toronto time; “close” windows use each day’s Google closing time. Prices are CAD before tax and tip, as printed.
- `venues.json` was rebuilt for Guelph only with `mergeArea` (the same code `npm run areas` runs): 91 venues, 44 with deals. The Guelph case in `src/lib/areas.test.ts` passes; the only failing case there is king-west’s, which is being worked on separately.

## Conflicts and caveats worth knowing

- **Trappers Alley’s hours.** Partytown’s site says Trappers and the Tap House open at noon (Mon, Thu–Sat to midnight; Sun, Tue, Wed to 6 pm) and runs $1 wings 2–5 pm every day, but Google lists Trappers as a 9 pm–2 am nightclub. The window is loaded with a note to check before going early. The site’s line that Trappers is “famous for $1 beers every Monday and Thursday” is old marketing copy with no times, so it is held back (Table B). Tabu (private rentals only) and The Palace are listed as closed on the same site.
- **McCabe’s Tuesday.** McCabe’s own page says $1 off tacos on Tuesdays; Cheap Eats (from an email with the pub, Aug 2026) says $4 off burgers on Tuesdays as well as Sundays. The venue page is loaded, with a note.
- **Baker Street Station’s** specials come from its own Instagram posts embedded on its site, dated May 2025 (half-price wings Mondays, Toonie Tuesday $2 oysters, fried chicken and donuts with $2 off all draft, half-price cocktails Wednesdays). Cheap Eats confirmed Monday and Tuesday with the staff in April 2026 and adds $7 Wellington pints on Mondays.
- **Bad Luck’s** Karaoke Sundays poster ($5 menu, $5 mixed drinks and domestic bottles, 5 pm–2 am) was uploaded in November 2023 but is still on its weekly-deals page; Cheap Eats re-checked it in September 2026.
- **Kelseys, State & Main, Royal Electric and East Side Mario’s** have changed prices since Cheap Eats last checked them (Kelseys burgers from $12 not $10, Michelob not Coors Light on Thursdays; State & Main’s happy hour now starts at $5; Royal Electric’s Sunday oysters are $2 not $1; East Side Mario’s Wednesday is now $15.99 pasta or pizza). The venues’ current pages are used.
- **Boston Pizza (Woodlawn)** is graded `reported`: its Guelph page shows no specials, and the week of deals comes from the general manager via Cheap Eats (Aug 2026). The chain’s own site agrees on Pasta Tuesday but says “from $11.99” where the GM says $15.99 gourmet pasta.
- **Symposium’s** happy hour (3–6 pm and 9 pm–close, house wine and PBR draught) has no published price; it is loaded with no prices and a note.
- **The WORKS** happy hour is chain-wide “at participating locations”; the Guelph store page lists a Happy Hour menu, so it is loaded with that note. The Guelph WORKS closes at 9 pm (10 pm Fri–Sat), so the 8 pm–close window is short.
- **Piatto (153 Wyndham St N)** is on reduced hours because of construction (Thu 5–9 pm, Fri–Sat noon–10 pm, Sun noon–8 pm) per its Guelph page; Google’s hours don’t reflect this.
- **Shakespeare Arms and Piper Arms** share the same specials format (Asian Night, 99¢/90¢ wing Tuesdays, $5 domestic pints), which suggests common ownership; they are listed separately.
- **Possible closures or changes:** Einstein’s Café now opens for weekday lunch only (11 am–2 pm). Wrinkly Bear Brewing (355 Elmira Rd N) isn’t in Google’s sweep and its unit was listed for sale, so it was not added. Fionn MacCool’s (Stone Rd) closed in 2023 ([GuelphToday](https://www.guelphtoday.com/local-news/after-10-years-south-end-barrestaurant-has-closed-7230865)).
