# Four Ossington venues publish verifiable happy hours

Of the 29 Ossington Avenue venues checked on October 26, 2026, only four publish a time-boxed happy hour on their own channels that can be verified. **Azhar Kitchen + Bar** runs Mon–Fri 4–6 pm and Sat–Sun 3–5 pm, with $5 Burdock cans, $10 spritzes, $10 and $15 plates and half-price bottles. **Central** runs daily 2–5 pm plus a Sun–Thu 9 pm–close late window, with $4 sliders and $5 highballs. **Manita** runs Mon–Thu 3–6 pm, with $2.50 oysters, $19 burger and fries and $7 draft. **Bar Libretto** runs 4–6 pm and 9 pm–late but publishes no happy-hour prices. **The Haifa Room** has no happy hour, but its own July 2026 menus carry two standing specials: **$6 arak all day, every day** and **half-off wine bottles on Tuesdays**. The app should store these as day specials rather than happy hours. **La Cubana closed on June 28, 2026**, **Superpoint has been closed since August 24, 2025**, and Bar Habana, the back room inside La Cubana, almost certainly closed with it. Four tracked venues (Favorites Thai, Sweaty Betty's, Reposado and Bar Koukla) have only undated, third-party or booking-tag claims, which should stay hidden until confirmed. The other 20 show no happy hour in any source that could be reached. No venue on the strip outside the tracked list has a well-documented happy hour. Bar Roma and The Painted Lady rest on a single roundup first published in June 2025, and the best-documented nearby deals are at the Queen and Dundas corners. One limitation applies to everything below: Instagram and most OpenTable pages could not be read, and small Ossington bars often announce deals there. "No happy hour found" means none appeared in any source that could be reached. It does not prove the venue has none.

## Venue menus fix the prices, but three time windows still conflict

**Azhar has the strongest record on the strip.** Its homepage and dedicated happy-hour page both give **Monday to Friday 4–6 pm and Saturday and Sunday 3–5 pm**. The homepage adds "$5 Burdock cans" and half-off selected bottles, and lists holiday closures for Family Day and Victoria Day on dates that match the 2026 calendar ([Azhar](https://azhartoronto.com)). An aggregator copy of the same menu, stamped "last updated 10/24/26", matches it item for item ([UltimateHappyHours](https://ultimatehappyhours.com/location/toronto-on/)). Two older figures are superseded and should be ignored. Fringinto gives the weekdays as Tue–Fri, from a list first published in June 2025 ([Fringinto](https://www.fringinto.com/best-happy-hours-toronto/)). An older OpenTable locale cache gives the weekday window as 5–6:30 pm ([OpenTable .de](https://www.opentable.de/r/azhar-kitchen-and-bar-toronto-1)). The conflicts that remain all come from Azhar's own OpenTable page, which says its menu was last updated September 3, 2026. That page adds a **drinks-only 10–11 pm session on Fridays and Saturdays**. It also lists **Martini Mondays at $10 a martini** under two different windows: 4–10 pm in the Experiences section and 5–9:30 pm in the Offers section ([OpenTable Azhar](https://www.opentable.ca/r/azhar-kitchen-and-bar-toronto-1)). The same OpenTable copy advertises "$5 snacks", but the cheapest food on the venue's current menu is $10, so prices should come from the venue page. One internal inconsistency points the other way. Azhar's site gives a 5 pm weekday opening, which contradicts its own 4 pm happy-hour start, while OpenTable's 4 pm opening fits it. The site's opening-hours block is probably the stale part.

**Central's afternoon window is settled, and its late-night window is not.** Central is a Calgary chain whose Toronto location at 114 Ossington opened July 3, 2025 ([centralrestaurants.ca locations](https://centralrestaurants.ca/locations); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/18/3101308/0/en/Central-Ossington-Opens-July-3-Toronto-s-Newest-Spot-for-Food-Cocktails-and-Culture.html)). Its Toronto menu page states **"Daily 2pm-5pm" and "Sun-Thu 9pm-close"** ([Central menu](https://centralrestaurants.ca/toronto-ossington/menu)). Opening coverage from June 2025 used the same Sun–Thu 9 pm framing ([Daily Hive, Jun 27, 2025](https://dailyhive.com/toronto/central-taps-toronto)). OpenTable instead says "Daily 9:00 pm–12:00 am" ([OpenTable Central](https://www.opentable.ca/r/central-restaurants-toronto-ossington-toronto)). blogTO's September 2025 roundup gives 10 pm–close, Sun–Wed ([blogTO, Sept 2025](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2025/09/happy-hours-toronto-september/)). That roundup is the most-cited figure and has the least support. The app should use the venue's Sun–Thu 21:00–24:00 and flag Friday and Saturday as disputed. The opening coverage also advertised **$4 braised pork carnitas tacos**, which are no longer on the menu. Roundup menus drift within months. **Manita's deal is the clearest discount on the strip, because its menu prints the regular prices beside it.** The Ossington menu PDF, created September 21, 2026, lists "HAPPY HOUR / Monday thru Thursday / *excluding holidays / 3 pm - 6 pm" ([Manita PDF](https://www.manita.ca/s/OSS-MENUS-2026.pdf)). The same file shows how big the cuts are. The **burger and fries drops to $19 from the $31 regular Manita Burger**. **Oysters at $2.50 each come to $15 for six, against $24 for a regular half dozen**. The **$7 draft compares with $9** for the regular Estrella Damm or Complot IPA. One caveat: the PDF has several columns, and the "Draft 7" line sits between the happy-hour wines and the next page header, so it is slightly less certain than the other items. **Bar Libretto confirms its windows but keeps its prices off the menu.** The venue page reads "Happy hour: 4-6pm | Late night: 9pm-late". ([Bar Libretto](https://www.pizzerialibretto.com/bar-libretto/); [menu PDF](https://pizzerialibretto.agencydominion.net/uploads/2026/02/BarLibretto_Menu.pdf)). Launch coverage describes "daily" happy hours running 4–6 and 9–11 pm across the Wed–Sat schedule the bar opened with ([blogTO, Feb 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/02/pizza-libretto-ossington-toronto); [Streets of Toronto, Feb 23, 2026](https://streetsoftoronto.com/food/restaurants/this-pizza-spot-just-became-the-coolest-late-night-hangout-on-ossington/)). Sunday service from 4 to 11 pm was added later. No source says whether happy hour runs on Sundays. Until the terms are published, the app can only show regular prices as context: 5 oz spritzes and 3 oz martinis at $12, a 5 oz house wine at $8, draft beer at $8–$9 and pizzas at $16–$22. A sitewide Pizzeria Libretto banner offers 50% off the Bambini menu "during Happy Hour" until October 31, but it names no location, so it should not be attached to Bar Libretto.

**The Haifa Room should be modelled as standing specials, not a happy hour.** Its dinner and lunch menu images, both titled 07.25.26, print "$6 ARAK — ALL DAY EVERYDAY" and "1/2 OFF Wine BOTTLES TUESDAYS ALL DAY!" ([dinner menu](https://thehaifaroom.com/s/NEW-DINNER-2.png); [lunch menu](https://www.thehaifaroom.com/s/NEW-LUNCH-1.png)). A diner review on OpenTable, seen only as a search snippet, mentions the Tuesday wine deal ([OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com/r/the-haifa-room-toronto)). A "happy hours right now" query will match the arak during every open hour. That is accurate, but it will crowd the results unless the app ranks time-limited deals above all-day ones.

## Undated posts and booking tags fall short of a listing

**Favorites Thai is the only weak claim that comes from the venue itself.** Its homepage feed shows "Join us for Wine Wednesday 🍷 50% off select bottles all night!" next to a separate post about patio-only Singha and $10 wine, with "Service starts at 6pm!" ([myfavethai.com](https://www.myfavethai.com/)). Neither post has a calendar date, and the patio offer is tied to a one-off oyster-shucking event. The menu page lists only a "Rotating wine & beer list!" with no prices ([myfavethai.com/menu](https://www.myfavethai.com/menu)). Wine Wednesday is plausible as a recurring Wednesday 18:00–22:00 special. It needs one confirmation before launch, and the patio deal should never be stored as recurring.

**Sweaty Betty's and Reposado rest on text that appears to be years old.** The only specific Sweaty Betty's terms, "weeknights between 5 and 7 pm, highballs and pints are a dollar off", come from an undated Yellow Pages patio guide. Its "©2026" footer describes the website, not the article ([Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.ca/pl/the-top-patios-in-toronto-west-end-edition-P3866)). Apple Maps and an Evendo guide both flag "happy hour" at the bar, but neither gives terms ([Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?place-id=I1B19A131D9DEA9EA); [Evendo](https://evendo.com/locations/canada/golden-horseshoe/bar/sweaty-betty-s)), and the bar's own site returned HTTP 429. Aggregator update stamps can also mislead. A TopTorontoClubs page marked "Updated June 2026" lists drink prices that exactly match a 2018 blogTO listing ([TopTorontoClubs](https://toptorontoclubs.com/clubs/sweaty-bettys-bar-toronto/); [blogTO](https://www.blogto.com/bars/sweaty_bettys/)). Reposado's "Tequila Tuesdays… $6 Palomas" comes from a directory whose newest review is from 2013 and which puts the bar in Scarborough ([Yabsta](https://toronto.yabsta.com/listing/view/116925/reposado-bar-lounge)). The most recent sign that it is operating is an April 2025 Spotted by Locals listing ([Spotted by Locals](https://spottedbylocals.com/toronto/reposado-bar-lounge)).

**Booking-platform tags conflict with the venues' own menus, and the menus should win.** Bar Koukla's OpenTable listing includes "Happy Hour" among its features, but its official page on the Mamakas site lists no happy hour at all ([OpenTable UK](https://www.opentable.co.uk/r/koukla-toronto-2); [mamakas.ca](https://mamakas.ca/locations/bar-koukla/)). Paris Paris carries a "Promoción nocturna" (late-night promotion) tag on its Mexican OpenTable mirror, yet its Fall 2026 menus, including an All Day PDF created September 10, 2026, contain no time-limited pricing ([OpenTable MX](https://www.opentable.com.mx/r/paris-paris-toronto); [All Day PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6539bc5cf1b0c2065ce660f7/6aa33853b637a64c4c6cddf1_Paris%20Paris%20All%20Day%20Fall%202026.pdf)). Mickey Limbo's has only a general mention of "classic beer-and-shot specials", with no days, times or prices ([VeryToronto](https://www.verytoronto.com/places/mickey-limbos)). The app should also avoid labelling set menus as deals. Don't Tell's Thursday 6-course tasting at $45 ([OpenTable](https://www.opentable.ca/r/dont-tell-toronto)), Mamakas' $55 weekend lunch prix fixe ([mamakas.ca](https://mamakas.ca/locations/mamakas-ossington/)) and Bar Koukla's $75 Géfsi menu are regular-priced set menus. Côte de Boeuf's $1 "buck a shuck" oysters ([Brosenose, Jan 2015](https://brosenose.com/2015/01/toronto-oysters-at-cote-de-boeuf.html)) and its Thu–Sun "Baron Rouge Nights" ([Toronto Life](https://torontolife.com/food/ossington-butcher-shop-cote-de-boeuf-now-also-snack-bar-sometimes/)) are about a decade old and must be dropped. The venue's old website domain, cdbossington.com, now serves unrelated adult content, so the app must never link to it.

## La Cubana and Superpoint are closed; nine more need a phone call

**Two tracked venues are confirmed closed.** La Cubana announced in early June 2026 that its Ossington location would close, and the last day of service was **June 28, 2026** ([blogTO, Jun 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/06/la-cubana-toronto-closing/); [inSauga](https://www.insauga.com/4-restaurants-that-closed-in-toronto-in-june-2026/)). By September every permanent La Cubana location had shut ([blogTO, Sept 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/09/la-cubana-toronto-closed/)), and **Company Bagel** was announced for 92 Ossington ([blogTO, Sept 2026](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/09/company-bagel-toronto/)). No source mentions Bar Habana, but it operated as a back room inside La Cubana's space ([blogTO, 2023](https://blogto.com/bars/bar-habana-toronto)), so it should be treated as closed. **Superpoint** served its last pizza on **August 24, 2025**, posting "THANKS 4 COMING. ## Conclusion

Ossington's happy-hour supply comes from a narrow group of operators. Central is a chain expansion, Bar Libretto is a 2026 rebrand built around late nights, Manita is a café-grocer, and Azhar is a mid-priced Middle Eastern room. The destination restaurants that define the strip all publish full, dated 2026 menus with no happy hour: Paris Paris, Linny's, Union, Mamakas and La Banane. For these venues, "no happy hour" is a defensible data point and should be shown with confidence, not hidden as missing data. The data model needs more than a yes/no field. It should cover time-boxed windows, all-day standing specials (Haifa's arak, Libretto's and Favorites' bottle nights), a "happy hour exists, terms unpublished" state for Bar Libretto, holiday exclusions for Manita, and pour sizes for Central. A simple acceptance test follows from the tables. At 16:30 on a non-holiday Monday such as the research date, the app should show Azhar (with Martini Mondays flagged medium), Central, Manita and Haifa's arak. It should show nothing from Bar Libretto, which is closed on Mondays.

The reliability ranking of the sources is the main lesson for maintaining the data. Dated venue files, such as Manita's September 2026 PDF and Haifa's July 2026 menu images, beat undated venue web pages. Those beat booking platforms, which in turn beat roundups. Every roundup cross-checked here contained at least one error: blogTO's Central late window, Fringinto's Azhar weekdays and its closed Electric Bill, OpenTable's "$5 snacks" and its Koukla tag. Third-party aggregation is useful for finding venues, not for publishing prices. The highest-value next checks are Instagram or a phone call for Bar Libretto's happy-hour prices and Sunday terms, the Favorites Thai and Sweaty Betty's leads, and the open status of the nine venues with shaky evidence. On the evidence here, a monthly re-check of the five Table A venues would catch most drift before users notice it.
